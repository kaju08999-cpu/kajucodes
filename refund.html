Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Important: Please review the product description carefully before completing a purchase because KajuCodes primarily provides digital products.

1. Digital Products

KajuCodes primarily sells digital study materials, notes and e-books. Because digital products may be delivered electronically after successful payment, purchases are generally non-refundable once the digital product has been delivered or accessed.

2. Eligible Refund Situations

A refund request may be considered in situations such as:

  • Payment was successfully completed but the purchased digital product was not delivered due to a technical problem.
  • The same order was accidentally charged more than once.
  • A significant technical issue prevents access to the purchased digital product and the issue cannot reasonably be resolved.

3. Refund Request Period

Customers should contact KajuCodes within 7 days of the transaction if they believe they have a valid refund issue.

4. Refund Review

Each refund request will be reviewed based on the transaction details and the circumstances of the request. Additional information may be requested to verify the transaction.

5. Duplicate Payments

If a customer is charged more than once for the same product due to a technical or payment issue, the duplicate transaction may be eligible for a refund after verification.

6. Failed Transactions

If a payment fails but an amount is temporarily debited from the customer's account, the transaction may be subject to the payment provider's normal reversal or refund process.

7. Cancellation

Cancellation requests for digital products may be considered only before the product has been delivered or accessed, where technically and operationally possible.

8. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, it will normally be processed through the applicable payment method or payment provider according to the provider's processing procedures.

9. Contact for Refunds

To request assistance regarding a payment, refund or delivery issue, contact:

Email: kajucodes@gmail.com

Please include your order/payment reference, purchase date, product name and a brief description of the issue.
